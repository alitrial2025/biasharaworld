The Kotlin/Compose app and Next.js website now implement the same twenty local demo capabilities. Start on Android at **More → Explore all 20 business tools**, or on the website at **Business tools → All business tools** (`/dashboard/tools`). The original sales, inventory, customers, suppliers and reports remain accessible.

## Data and transaction rules

- Integer minor units drive new checkout, discount, refund and register calculations. Order-wide deductions and incremental refunds allocate rounding deterministically. Historical receipts snapshot original prices and bundle components.
- A held basket or draft document does not change stock. Checkout revalidates stock and reward balance; invalid commands leave the workspace unchanged. A branch switch parks an active basket at its original branch.
- Expired batches and confirmed/packing reservations reduce sellable availability. Bundle stock derives from components. Dispatch consumes customer-order stock once; delivery and payment do not consume it again.
- Transfer dispatch removes source stock; receiving adds destination stock once. Returning a dispatched transfer records source stock restoration. Counts reject a stale starting snapshot.
- Paid delivered orders create a receipt, sales entry, cash-register entry where a session is open, and reward event. Return records reverse payment/reward portions and feed the sales summary. Delivery fees are shown separately; product returns refund the product's original net amount.
- Register totals contain only cash events recorded during that branch's open session. Sample historical sales do not inflate the opening float.
- Android snapshots business data to Preferences DataStore; web uses `biasharaos.demo.v2` localStorage and migrates v1 sample records. Malformed saved data and unavailable storage have recovery messages. Legacy aggregate receipts remain visible without inventing returnable line items.

## Presentation and integration boundaries

Inter/Manrope, emerald actions, orange OS branding, product photography, pastel metric cards and compact lists continue across both platforms. Dark surfaces and relative web typography support the display preferences. The shared Kiswahili phrase catalogue is generated by `python3 tools/generate-locales.py`; names, product identities and SKUs remain original. Editorial/legal articles and some historical sample narratives remain English; a professional language review is still appropriate before launch.

All financial and fulfilment actions are local previews. There is no real authentication, customer identity check, tax certification, payment collection/refund, lending, email/SMS, camera decoder, supplier/driver connection, live AI or server sync. Web and Android do not exchange data. Android works from bundled assets/local data; a loaded web session retains edits, but offline cold-start caching is not implemented. Print controls invoke the browser/Android print system; physical printers and camera/device integrations were not verified in this cloud machine.
